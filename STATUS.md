## 🔍 Why Is This Happening?

### The Technical Reason
GitHub Pages is a security-sensitive feature that must be explicitly enabled by repository administrators. Even though:
- The code exists ✅
- The workflows are configured ✅  
- Everything is pushed ✅

...GitHub won't deploy anything until Pages is enabled in Settings.

### The Fix Is Simple
It's literally just clicking a dropdown in Settings and selecting a source. That's it.
